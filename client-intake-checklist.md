# 73-Hour Clock Production Intake Checklist

Execute these verification and asset collection steps before rewriting any repository boilerplate code:

## Phase 1: Asset Extraction (< 2 Hours from Payment)
- [ ] Verify baseline client upfront payment of $249.00 processed successfully.
- [ ] Confirm target domain name ownership credentials or request Cloudflare transfer access.
- [ ] Extract raw logo assets (Prefer vector .svg formats, fallback to transparent background .png).
- [ ] Secure 3 to 5 high-resolution product or location images for the core gallery section.

## Phase 2: Copywriting Requirements (Text Blocks)
- [ ] Core Headlines: Collect the exact business name, tagline, and founding year.
- [ ] Operating Schedule: Document precise open/close hours for weekdays and weekends.
- [ ] Strategic Contact Matrix: Verify physical storefront address, public phone line, and customer service email.

## Phase 3: Dynamic Frame Specifications (The 5 Tools)
- [ ] Restaurant Menu: Gather initial 3-5 structural dishes with flat prices and clear item descriptions.
- [ ] Retail Shop: Catalog baseline products, active inventory status, and standard retail pricing levels.
- [ ] Announcements: Draft the inaugural welcome post or seasonal operation update.
- [ ] Events: Document any immediate upcoming workshop dates, market dates, or holiday reservation schedules.
